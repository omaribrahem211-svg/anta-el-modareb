# MLS 2026 game data and competition

Roster snapshot: 22 November 2026. Thirty clubs and 832 supported player biographies. Unresolved membership or birth-date conflicts are excluded. The source URLs for each included player are retained in `src/data/mls2026RosterFacts.json`.

Real identity facts are separate from game parameters. Ratings, attributes, market values, wages, finances and club reputation are game balancing. Nineteen positions are adapted to the engine's supported positions. Unknown preferred feet are estimated and labelled. Additional squad depth is generated by the game and labelled as fictional in the player's name and profile. It is not part of the 832-player dataset.

The adapted regular season has 34 matches per club, 17 home and 17 away. Each club plays the other fourteen clubs in its conference twice and six different clubs from the other conference once. Opponents and dates are adapted to the game's existing career calendar, not claimed to be the real fixture schedule.

The playoffs use the top nine clubs from each conference: 8 v 9 wild card; Round One best-of-three with no aggregate score; conference semifinals and finals; MLS Cup. Wild card and Round One tied games go directly to penalties. Later tied games have extra time and then penalties. Playoffs do not change regular-season standings. MLS Cup and Supporters' Shield are separate awards. Some disciplinary tiebreak details and the final draw are simplified for simulation. North American continental competitions are not implemented, and MLS clubs are not routed into African, European or Asian continental competitions.

League and match snapshots preserve the playoff metadata. Existing careers and evolved squads are kept, not replaced by the new seed rosters. National-team country entries remain unique when USA and Canada become available.

## Test coverage

The MLS roster, schedule and playoff scripts check identity/provenance, stable IDs, deterministic conversion, schedule invariants, bracket progression, best-of-three hosting, extra time, saved brackets, Cup versus Shield and full-season simulation. The browser integration test mounts the real game state and checks mobile tables, Messi, a live playoff, uncommitted-result reload and commit, sibling results, the next series games, and 105/120-minute extra-time restore. These checks do not replace native Android gameplay testing.
